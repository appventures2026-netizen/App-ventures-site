Privacy Policy

Scope

Welcome to Vale's Privacy Policy. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Vale ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, protects, and shares information when you use our guided breathing mobile application and related services (the "App" or "Services"), available on the Apple App Store.

If you do not agree with this Privacy Policy, please do not use our Services.

Overview

Vale is a guided breathing app. It plays timed breathing exercises, keeps a history of the sessions you complete, can remind you to practise, and — only if you choose to connect it — reads recent trends from Apple Health to suggest when a session might help.

  • Vale has no user accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, no password, and no email address required to use the App.
  • Vale has no servers of its own. Your session history, reminders, mood entries, and preferences are stored only on your device.
  • Your Apple Health data never leaves your device. It is read on-device, used on-device, and never transmitted, shared, or used for advertising.
  • Vale does not publish anything about you to the internet, and there is no social or sharing feed.

Note: Because Vale stores your practice data locally rather than in the cloud, that data is not backed up by us and cannot be recovered by us. It is included in your device's own iCloud or encrypted local backups, and deleting the App deletes it.

1. No Account Registration

Unlike most apps, Vale does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • A password or any login credential
  • Social or third-party sign-in identities

You can optionally enter a first name during onboarding so the App can greet you. That name is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide (stored on your device)

The following is created by you inside the App and saved locally in the App's own storage. We have no access to it:

  • Completed session records — which exercise, when, how long, and how many rounds
  • Optional mood ratings you record after a session
  • Breathing reminders you schedule (time, days of the week, and chosen exercise)
  • Your preferences — haptics, ambient sound, phase cue tones, and onboarding goals
  • An optional first name, and your acknowledgement of exercise safety notices

2.2 Apple Health Data (optional, on-device only)

Connecting Apple Health is entirely optional and off by default. If you grant permission, Vale reads the following through Apple's HealthKit framework:

  • Heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleep analysis

Vale reads roughly the last 14 days of these samples, averages them into daily trend points on your device, and uses them to suggest a suitable breathing session. If you complete a session, Vale writes Mindful Minutes back to Apple Health.

Health data commitment: Health and fitness data read through HealthKit is processed entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, never used for advertising or marketing, never used for data mining, and never sold. You can revoke Vale's Health access at any time in the iOS Health app under Sharing > Apps.

2.3 Usage Information

Vale does not include any analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or behavioural tracking SDK. We do not collect information about which screens you view, which features you use, how long you spend in the App, or your interaction patterns. We receive no usage reports about you at all.

Apple may independently provide us with aggregated, anonymised App Store metrics (such as total downloads and aggregate crash counts) if you have enabled sharing of analytics with developers in your iOS settings. That data is aggregated by Apple and does not identify you.

2.4 Device & Advertising Data

Vale is supported by advertising in its free version. Our advertising partner, TopOn (together with the ad networks it mediates), may collect and process:

  • Device type, model, operating system, and app version
  • IP address, and coarse location inferred from it (Vale does not request or access GPS location)
  • The Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), only if you grant App Tracking Transparency permission
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks, and completions

If you decline the tracking prompt, Vale and its ad partners do not access the IDFA, and you will receive non-personalised advertising instead.

2.5 Billing Information

If you subscribe to Vale Pro, payment is processed by the Apple App Store and managed through our subscription partner, RevenueCat. We never see, collect, or store your payment card details. We receive only subscription status and purchase metadata — such as whether an entitlement is active, the product purchased, and renewal or expiry dates — associated with an anonymous RevenueCat identifier, not with your name or email.

2.6 Notifications

Breathing reminders are scheduled and delivered locally by your device. Vale does not operate a push notification server, does not register device push tokens, and does not send you remote marketing messages. Reminder content never leaves your device.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information described above to:

  • Run guided breathing sessions and keep your practice history and streaks on your device
  • Suggest a breathing exercise based on your recent Apple Health trends, where you have connected Health
  • Record completed sessions to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes
  • Deliver the local reminders you schedule
  • Unlock and maintain Vale Pro features for active subscribers
  • Temporarily unlock an individual exercise when you choose to watch a rewarded advertisement
  • Display advertising in the free version of the App
  • Respond to support requests you send us by email
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information to build a profile about you, and we do not use Apple Health data for any purpose other than the in-App suggestions and Mindful Minutes logging described above.

5. Permissions

Vale may request the following permissions. Each is optional, and the App's core breathing exercises work without any of them:

  • Apple Health (read): To read heart rate, HRV, resting heart rate, and sleep trends so the App can suggest a suitable session. Declining simply removes the personalised suggestions.
  • Apple Health (write): To log completed sessions as Mindful Minutes.
  • Notifications: To deliver the breathing reminders you schedule. You can disable these in iOS Settings at any time.
  • App Tracking Transparency: To allow personalised advertising in the free version. Declining results in non-personalised ads.
  • Background App Refresh: To receive Health updates in the background so trend-based suggestions stay current.

Vale does not request microphone, camera, photo library, contacts, or precise location access. The App plays audio but never records it.

6. Data Security

Your practice data is stored in the App's private storage area on your device, protected by iOS application sandboxing and your device passcode and encryption. Because this data is never transmitted to a server of ours, it is not exposed to the risks associated with cloud storage or data breaches on our side.

Data transmitted to Apple, RevenueCat, and our advertising partner is sent over encrypted connections. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted or stored through the Services.

4. Health & Safety — Please Read

Breathing exercises are not risk-free. Techniques offered in this App include voluntary hyperventilation and extended breath retention. These can cause dizziness, light-headedness, tingling, and fainting or loss of consciousness, sometimes without warning.

You agree that you will:

  • Never practise breath retention or power breathing in or near water, including in a bath, pool, or open water. Loss of consciousness in water can be fatal.
  • Never practise while driving, cycling, operating machinery, standing at height, or in any other situation where fainting could cause injury.
  • Practise seated or lying down, in a safe place where a fall could not harm you.
  • Stop immediately and breathe normally if you feel unwell, faint, or distressed.

Consult a qualified physician before using the App if you are pregnant, or if you have or may have any cardiovascular condition, high or low blood pressure, a history of stroke, epilepsy or a seizure disorder, asthma or another respiratory condition, glaucoma, a panic or anxiety disorder, or any other medical condition that may be affected by altered breathing.

Where the App displays a safety notice for a specific exercise, you agree to read it and to acknowledge it truthfully. Acknowledging a safety notice confirms that you have read and understood the risk and accept it voluntarily.
